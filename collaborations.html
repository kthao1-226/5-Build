Collaboration

Connect with other creatives to bring ideas to life.

Past Collaborations

  • Sunshine Pet Rescue (2023): Created a short video for the organization to promote adoption of pets.
  • Behind Those Eyes (2024): "Behind Those Eyes" a film raising awareness about mental health and expressing real-life experiences of individuals dealing with mental health challenges.
  • Mood (2025): An uprising music artist Solar, collaboration to create a music video for their latest single called "Mood".
  • Gina & Rick's Wedding (2025): Collaborated on a project with videographer Amy Luum, to capture a beautiful event in a cinematic manner.

Coming Soon Collaborations

  • Hanging In The Forest, releasing October 30th, 2025: Follows a series of mysterious events occurring in a dense forest, as a group of friends attempt to uncover the truth behind the strange occurrences.
  • Christmas In Griff, releasing December 12th, 2025: Collaboration with the University of Raven to produce a short film exploring local community stories about Christmas.
  • In The Rustling Leaves, releasing March 7th, 2026: Collaboration with animal enthusiast Mike Von, "In The Rustling Leaves" A documentary exploring the lives of local animals.
  • Sticky Notes releasing July 17th, 2026: A deep moving romance film following two high school students navigating love in times of where interracial relationships were impossible.
